Privacy Policy

Argus Business Intelligence™ (website, Windows app and Android app) · Last updated 23 November 2026

Argus Business Intelligence is a business management app (sales, production, inventory, finance, attendance) used by companies and their staff. Accounts are created by each company's administrator; people sign in with their Google account. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: your name, e-mail address and profile picture from your Google sign-in, your role and the company you belong to.
  • Business records you enter: enquiries, quotations, orders, invoices, stock, production, tasks, notes and similar records your company keeps in the app.
  • Attendance (only if your company uses AI Attendance): when you register your face, the app turns the camera picture into a set of numbers (a face pattern) on your device; only those numbers are stored, never the photo. Punch-in / punch-out times are stored with the attendance record.
  • Location (only for attendance punches, if your company turns on the work-area check): your location at the moment you punch in or out, to confirm you are at the work site. Location is not tracked in the background.
  • Camera: used only while you register your face, punch attendance or attach a photo; pictures are not uploaded unless you save them as an attachment.
  • Microphone: used only while you talk to the app's voice assistant; your speech is turned into text to answer you.
  • Questions to the AI assistant: the questions you type or say, with the business figures needed to answer them, are sent to our AI service provider to produce the answer.
  • Technical information: app version, device type and error reports, to keep the app working.

2. How we use it

  • To sign you in and show you only your own company's data.
  • To provide the app's features: records, reports, attendance, payroll, the AI assistant and notifications.
  • To keep the service secure, fix problems and improve it.

We do not sell your information and do not use it for advertising.

3. Who can see it

  • Your company: people in your company see records according to the access your administrator gives them. Data of one company is kept separate from every other company.
  • Service providers who run the service for us (cloud hosting and database, Google sign-in, e-mail delivery, payment links, the AI service provider), only to provide the service.
  • The law: when we are legally required to.

4. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored with access rules that limit every record to its company. Face patterns, salary and bank details can only be read by the server, never directly by another user's device.

5. How long we keep it

Records are kept while your company uses the app, or as long as your company needs them for legal and accounting reasons. When an account or company is deleted, its data is removed from the live system.

6. Your choices

  • You can refuse camera, microphone or location access in your phone's settings; only the feature that needs it will stop working.
  • You can ask your company administrator to correct or delete your information.
  • To delete your account and its data, see Delete your account.

7. Children

The app is meant for businesses and their staff. It is not meant for children under 18.

8. Changes

We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the latest version.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, contact your company's Argus Business Intelligence administrator, or raise a request in the app under Technical Support.

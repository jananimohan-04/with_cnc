Delete your account

Argus Business Intelligence™ — Android app, Windows app and website

Accounts in Argus Business Intelligence are created and managed by your company's administrator. You can ask for your account and its data to be deleted in either of these ways:

  1. Ask your administrator: your company's administrator can delete your account in the app under Administration → User Management.
  2. Raise a request in the app: sign in, open Technical Support, and create a ticket titled "Delete my account". We confirm and complete it within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your user account and sign-in link (name, e-mail, profile picture, role).
  • Your attendance face pattern and your personal settings.
  • Your personal items such as your vision, mission, goals and chat history with the assistant.

What may be kept

Business records that belong to your company (for example invoices, orders and attendance registers you were part of) are kept by your company for as long as it needs them for accounting and legal reasons; your name may stay on those records. If your whole company stops using the app, its data is deleted on request.

See also our Privacy Policy.
